 Frequently Asked Questions

📌 How do I reset my password?

Go to the login page and click on "Forgot Password". You’ll receive an email with reset instructions.

📌 Where can I manage my account settings?

Click on your profile icon at the top-right, then select "Settings" to manage preferences, email, and security.

📌 How do I publish a new article?

Navigate to the "Dashboard" and click on "Create Article". Fill out the form and hit submit.

📌 Can I collaborate with others on articles?

Currently, collaboration is in beta. You can request access by contacting support.
